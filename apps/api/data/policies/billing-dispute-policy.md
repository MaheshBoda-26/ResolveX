# Billing Dispute Policy

## Overview
This policy governs customer-initiated billing disputes and chargeback handling.

## Duplicate Charge Window
- **Reporting Window**: 30 days from charge date
- **Auto-Refund**: Duplicate charges within 30 days auto-refunded (≤ $50 threshold)
- **Evidence**: Invoice ID, charge date, amount, payment method last 4 digits

## Evidence Requirements
### Required for All Disputes
- Customer statement describing the issue
- Invoice/charge reference numbers
- Date range of disputed charges

### Additional by Type
- **Duplicate Charge**: Bank statement showing both charges
- **Unauthorized Charge**: Police report (if fraud) or written statement
- **Service Not Rendered**: Service logs, uptime reports, communication records
- **Incorrect Amount**: Contract/quote showing agreed price

## Processing Timeline
1. **Acknowledgment**: Within 4 hours (business hours)
2. **Investigation**: 3-5 business days
3. **Resolution**: Within 10 business days
4. **Notification**: Written decision to customer

## Chargeback Handling
- **Response Window**: 7 days from notification
- **Evidence Package**: Transaction logs, customer communications, policy references
- **Win Rate Target**: > 80% for valid disputes
- **Monitoring**: Monthly chargeback rate review (threshold: 0.5%)

## Escalation
- > $500 disputes → Billing manager
- Chargeback disputes → Finance director
- Pattern disputes (3+ in 90 days) → Security review
