# Subscription Cancellation Policy

## Overview
This policy defines the process, notice periods, and proration rules for subscription cancellations.

## Notice Periods

### Monthly Plans
- **Notice Required**: None (cancel anytime)
- **Effective Date**: End of current billing cycle
- **Access**: Full access until cycle end

### Annual Plans
- **Notice Required**: 30 days before renewal
- **Effective Date**: Next renewal date
- **Early Cancellation**: Allowed with 50% prorated refund of unused months

### Enterprise Plans
- **Notice Required**: 60 days per contract terms
- **Effective Date**: Contract end date
- **Early Termination**: Subject to contract early-termination clause

## Proration Rules
- Monthly: No refund for unused days in current cycle
- Annual (early cancel): 50% of unused months refunded
- Enterprise: Per signed contract terms

## Process
1. Customer initiates cancellation via portal or support
2. System validates notice period eligibility
3. Confirmation email sent with effective date
4. Access continues until effective date
5. Final invoice generated (if applicable)
6. Data export initiated per retention policy

## Reactivation
- Within 30 days: Full restoration, same plan/pricing
- 30-90 days: Restoration at current pricing
- > 90 days: New subscription required
