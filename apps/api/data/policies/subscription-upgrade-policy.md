# Subscription Upgrade Policy

## Overview
This policy defines eligibility, process, and pricing rules for subscription tier changes.

## Eligibility
- Active subscription in good standing
- No past-due invoices
- Account not under security review

## Upgrade Types

### Same-Tier Upgrades (Auto-Approved)
- **Examples**: Basic → Pro monthly, Pro → Enterprise monthly
- **Processing**: Immediate provisioning
- **Billing**: Prorated charge for remainder of current cycle
- **Effective Date**: Immediate

### Cross-Tier Upgrades (Review Required)
- **Examples**: Basic → Enterprise, Pro → Enterprise
- **Processing**: Sales/Success team review within 24 hours
- **Billing**: Prorated; may require new contract
- **Effective Date**: Upon approval or next cycle (customer choice)

### Downgrades
- **Processing**: Take effect at next billing cycle
- **Proration**: No refund for unused portion of current cycle
- **Feature Access**: Current tier features remain until cycle end
- **Data Retention**: Enterprise features data exported per data retention policy

## Proration Rules
- Upgrade: Charge = (New Price - Old Price) × (Days Remaining / Days in Cycle)
- Downgrade: No credit; new price applies at next renewal
- Cycle change (monthly ↔ annual): Full proration applies

## Enterprise Upgrades
- Require signed MSA/Quote
- Implementation timeline: 5-10 business days
- Dedicated CSM assigned post-signature
