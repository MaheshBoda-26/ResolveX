# Refund Policy

## Overview
This policy governs refund processing for ResolveX customers. Refunds are categorized by amount thresholds with escalating approval requirements.

## Thresholds and Approval Requirements

### Automatic Refunds (≤ $50)
- **Eligibility**: Duplicate charges, billing errors, service outages > 4 hours
- **Processing**: Automatic approval within 24 hours
- **Notification**: Customer notified via email upon processing
- **Reversal**: Original payment method credited within 5-10 business days

### Manager Review ($50 - $500)
- **Eligibility**: Service dissatisfaction, partial service delivery, plan change within 14 days
- **Processing**: Manager review within 48 hours
- **Evidence Required**: Customer statement, service logs, billing records
- **Escalation**: If denied, customer may request director review

### Director Approval (> $500)
- **Eligibility**: Annual plan cancellations, enterprise contract disputes, cumulative refunds > $500
- **Processing**: Director review within 5 business days
- **Evidence Required**: Full case history, contract terms, usage analytics
- **Final Decision**: Director decision is final; no further internal appeal

## Exclusions
- Refunds requested > 90 days after charge (except duplicate charges)
- Custom development work delivered per specification
- Enterprise contracts with separate SLA terms
