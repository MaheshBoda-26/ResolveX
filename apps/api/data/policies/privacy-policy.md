# Privacy Policy

## Overview
This policy governs data retention, customer rights, and privacy compliance for ResolveX.

## Data Collection

### Categories Collected
- **Account Data**: Name, email, company, role, phone (optional)
- **Billing Data**: Payment method tokens, billing address, tax ID, invoice history
- **Usage Data**: Feature usage, API calls, login timestamps, session duration
- **Support Data**: Chat transcripts, ticket history, attachments, recordings
- **Technical Data**: IP address, user agent, device fingerprint, error logs

### Collection Methods
- Direct input (forms, API)
- Automated (analytics, logs)
- Third-party (payment processors, identity providers)
- Support interactions (chat, email, phone)

## Data Retention

### Active Account
- Account data: Duration of account + 30 days
- Billing data: 7 years (tax/legal compliance)
- Usage data: 2 years (aggregated after 90 days)
- Support data: 3 years
- Technical logs: 90 days (security logs: 1 year)

### Closed Account
- Account data: 30 days grace, then anonymized
- Billing data: 7 years (legal requirement)
- Usage data: Anonymized immediately
- Support data: 3 years from closure
- Backups: 90 days max

### Legal Hold
- All retention suspended on legal hold notice
- Legal team authorizes release
- Audit trail of hold/release actions

## Customer Rights (GDPR/CCPA Compliance)

### Right of Access
- Self-service dashboard: Real-time
- Complete export: API/portal (30-day SLA)
- Includes: All personal data, processing purposes, recipients, retention periods

### Right to Rectification
- Self-service profile updates: Immediate
- Support-assisted corrections: 7-day SLA
- Billing data corrections: 14-day SLA (audit trail)

### Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")
- Eligibility: No active subscription, no legal hold, no outstanding balance
- Process: 30-day SLA from verified request
- Scope: Personal identifiers; billing records retained (legal basis)
- Confirmation: Erasure certificate provided

### Right to Restrict Processing
- Marketing opt-out: Immediate
- Analytics opt-out: Immediate (aggregated data retained)
- Support communications: Per-channel preferences

### Right to Data Portability
- Format: JSON (standard), CSV (on request)
- Delivery: Secure download link (7-day expiry)
- Scope: Account, usage, support data (not derived/inferred data)

### Right to Object
- Direct marketing: Absolute right
- Legitimate interest processing: Balancing test applied
- Automated decision-making: Human review on request

## Data Sharing

### Processors (DPAs in Place)
- Payment: Stripe (PCI DSS)
- Cloud: AWS/GCP (SOC 2)
- Communications: SendGrid, Twilio
- Analytics: PostHog (self-hosted option)
- Support: Freshworks

### Subprocessors
- Published registry updated quarterly
- 30-day notice for new subprocessors
- Customer objection right

### Law Enforcement
- Valid legal process required
- Narrow scope enforced
- Customer notification (unless gag order)
- Transparency report annually

## Children's Privacy
- No knowingly collected data from < 16
- Age verification on enterprise sign-up
- Parental consent workflow if detected

## International Transfers
- Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)
- Adequacy decisions (EU, UK, Canada, Japan)
- Transfer impact assessments documented
