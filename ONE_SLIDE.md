# ResolveX — Autonomous Customer Resolution Engine

## Problem
Customer support requests requiring actions (refunds, plan changes, account updates) force customers into multi-day ticket queues while agents manually coordinate across billing, subscription, and policy systems.

## Solution
ResolveX is an autonomous resolution engine that triages requests, routes to specialist agents, grounds decisions in policy via RAG, enforces safety through a deterministic Autonomy Gate, executes actions via Freshworks MCP, and verifies outcomes — resolving in seconds what takes humans days.

## Impact
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Time Saved** | 10 min hold + 2-3 day wait → **<30 seconds end-to-end** |
| **$ Saved** | $70/mo per customer via right-sized plans + agent hours freed for complex issues |
| **Satisfaction** | **100% autonomous resolution rate** (33/33 eval cases) with **0 unauthorized high-risk actions** |
